## 🔑 1. **Quereinsteiger (Career Switcher) Path — Your Easiest Door In**

### ✅ Why it works:

- Germany LOVES structured transitions ("Umschulung", "Quereinsteiger")
    
- Companies are open to career changers **if you have real projects**
    
- Often **no degree required**
    
- Entry is through:
    
    - Junior developer roles
        
    - Trainee programs
        
    - Internships
        
    - Werkstudent/part-time roles (even for non-students sometimes)
        
    - DevOps Support, QA, Testing → later switch to dev
        

### 🛠️ What to do:

- Apply on **Campusjäger**, **HeyJobs**, and **Quereinsteiger listings on Stepstone**
    
- Look for jobs that say:
    
    - “Berufseinsteiger”
        
    - “Quereinsteiger willkommen”
        
    - “Junior Webentwickler”
        
    - “Mit Bootcamp Erfahrung” (very few say this, but growing)
        

---

## 🧑‍💻 2. **Freelancing on Malt or Fiverr — Build Income + Experience**

### ✅ Why it’s powerful:

- Low barrier to entry
    
- You can start **immediately**
    
- Clients care more about outcome than degrees
    
- Builds portfolio + reputation
    
- Earn €200–€500 per month with just a few tasks
    

### 🛠️ What to do:

- Register Gewerbe
    
- Offer 2–3 services:
    
    - “I’ll build you a landing page”
        
    - “Fix CSS bugs”
        
    - “Connect website to CMS”
        

➡️ This gives you **real projects + testimonials** = rocket fuel for job apps.

---

## 💻 3. **Junior Developer Path — Longer-Term, More Stable**

### ✅ Why it’s harder:

- More competition
    
- Companies often want “2 years experience”
    
- But: if you show **polished portfolio, GitHub, and confidence**, they’ll often **ignore degree requirements**
    

### 🛠️ What to do:

- Build 3 solid projects:
    
    - E-commerce site (React + Next.js)
        
    - Blog with CMS (Sanity)
        
    - Portfolio site (Tailwind + Next)
        
- Apply to jobs through:
    
    - LinkedIn
        
    - Angel.co / EU-startups
        
    - Startups → outreach via cold DMs
        
- Prep for interviews slowly over time
