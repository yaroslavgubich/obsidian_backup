Certainly! Here's a condensed version of the problem-solving checklist with a focus on simplification, starting with cleanup and meditation as the first steps:

### Simplified Problem-Solving Checklist

#### 1. Cleanup
- [ ] Clear and organize your workspace to minimize distractions.
- [ ] Ensure all necessary materials and tools are readily available.(documentation, lectures, etc.)

#### 2. Meditation (10 Minutes)
- [ ] Engage in a 10-minute meditation session to clear your mind and enhance focus.
#### 3. Set a Timer
- [ ] Set a timer to prevent time leaks 
#### 4. Define and Break Down the Problem
- [ ] Read the problem statement carefully to fully understand it.
- [ ] Break the problem into smaller, manageable parts.
#### 5. Testing
 - [ ] Create tests 
 - [ ] run failing tests
 - [ ] identify what is end result
#### 6. Plan Your Approach
- [ ] For each part, brainstorm potential strategies.
- [ ] For each sub-task, outline a pseudo-code or flowchart to plan the logic and structure.
- [ ] Decide on the programming tools, libraries, methods or frameworks best suited for each part.

#### 7. Work in Focused Intervals
- [ ] Use the Pomodoro Technique (25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break). Set a timer.
- [ ] Keep a notepad for jotting down distractions or sudden insights during focus periods.
#### 8. Review and Adjust
- [ ] After each work interval, review and adjust your progress.
#### 9. Seek Feedback (Optional)
- [ ] Share your approach and partial solutions with peers or mentors for feedback.
- [ ] Incorporate useful feedback to refine your solution.
#### 10. Document and Share
- [ ] Document the problem, solution, and any insights gained.
- [ ] Share your findings with friends.
- [ ] Reflect on the problem-solving process to identify lessons learned.
